# FGND-0001. Intent and Uncertainty

Date: 2026-09-06
Last-reviewed: 2026-09-06
Tier: S
Status: Accepted

## Related

Root: FGND-0001

## Context

Reusable direction must survive incomplete knowledge without granting unlimited discretion to its executor.

## Decision

Intent and boundaries constrain decisions; mechanisms remain local unless essential to the objective.

R1 [3]: Work briefs MUST identify the intended outcome, its purpose, scope boundaries and the accountable decision owner.
R2 [3]: Executors MUST distinguish observed facts from assumptions and identify evidence that would invalidate a load-bearing assumption.
R3 [3]: Competing objectives MUST have an explicit priority so local adaptation preserves the principal outcome.

## Consequences

Teams can adapt implementation without treating uncertainty as permission to expand authority or scope.
